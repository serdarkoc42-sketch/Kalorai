# Kalorai Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası KalorAI – AI-Powered Nutrition Coach Last Updated: May 2026 🇬🇧 ENGLISH Privacy Policy Welcome to KalorAI (“the App”). Your privacy is important to us. KalorAI is designed with a privacy-focused approach. 1. Information Collection KalorAI does not collect, store, or share personal user data on external servers. All user information entered into the application is stored locally on the user's device only. This may include: Name or nickname Age Gender Height and weight Nutrition goals Meal tracking information 2. Meal Photos Photos uploaded by users for food analysis are processed only temporarily for analysis purposes. KalorAI does not permanently store uploaded meal photos on cloud servers. 3. Data Storage All application data is stored locally on the user’s device. KalorAI does not operate its own cloud database or remote user account system. If the app is deleted, locally stored data may also be removed from the device. 4. AI Features Some app features may use artificial intelligence technologies to analyze food images and provide nutritional estimates. Only the minimum required data is processed for these features. 5. Third-Party Services KalorAI may use limited third-party services necessary for app functionality, such as: Google Play Services Google Play Billing Anonymous crash reporting tools These services may collect technical information according to their own privacy policies. 6. Advertising The free version of the app may display advertisements. Third-party advertising providers may use anonymous identifiers for ad delivery. KalorAI itself does not collect personal advertising profiles. 7. Premium Features Premium subscriptions may provide access to: Unlimited AI analysis Advanced nutrition insights Additional tracking features Payments are securely handled through Google Play. 8. Children’s Privacy KalorAI is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. 9. Your Rights Because user data is stored locally on the device: Users can delete app data anytime Users can uninstall the app at any time Users maintain full control over their local data 10. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will appear on this page. 11.
